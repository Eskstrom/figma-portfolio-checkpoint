# Watch & Unlock — Screen gallery

[← Project overview](README.md) · [Interaction notes](WIREFRAMES.md)

All screens are grayscale concept wireframes. Token balances are scenario states, not a live account.

## TV-01 — Episode complete

**Spendable balance: 5 tokens.** Default focus: Play Episode 4. Right moves to the voluntary ad offer. No autoplay countdown in this wireframe.

![Episode complete](prototype/screens/tv-01.svg)

## TV-02 — Optional sponsor ad

**Spendable balance: 5 tokens.** Prototype simulation: use the review toolbar to complete the ad. Real earning requires confirmed completion. Exit preserves the 5-token balance.

![Optional sponsor ad](prototype/screens/tv-02.svg)

## TV-03 — Ad reward confirmed

**Spendable balance: 20 tokens.** Continue stays the easiest action. Scanning the QR code does not earn tokens; the optional 10-second demo does.

![Ad reward confirmed](prototype/screens/tv-03.svg)

## TV-04 — Continue on your phone
