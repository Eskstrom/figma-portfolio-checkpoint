## The problem and direction

Connect university outreach to a complete service experience: coupon distribution, a visit, point-of-sale validation, social amplification, repeat visits, and feedback. The blueprint distinguishes frontstage experiences from backstage work. Ecosystem maps examine participants and value flows.

## Artifacts and evidence

The original blueprint and both ecosystem-map versions remain in Figma. The existing checkpoint documents visual inspection without design edits. The public artifacts do not establish a launched partnership or measured improvement in visits or revenue.
