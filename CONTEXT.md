## FastMail kiosk

Link: [Fast mail Kiosk](https://www.figma.com/make/gc3I9jqgY0XMzbZ0SOaPKp/Fast-mail-Kiosk).

FastMail is a self-service parcel kiosk concept. Version 5 contains ten screens: Welcome, Destination, Package size, Weight, Package type, Collection method, Delivery speed and cost, Review, Payment, and Confirmation. It uses navy and teal styling, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono for prices and references, progress labels, large touch areas, validated recipient/street/apartment/city/state/postal fields, understandable handling choices, itemized estimates, editable review, demo-only payment, and a portfolio control panel.

Verified: empty required-field errors; complete sample address; package, weight, handling, collection, and speed choices; estimate changing from $19.99 to $29.99; review editing preserving the order; simulated decline and retry; successful demo receipt; start-over dismissal preserving data; confirmed reset clearing the session. Receipt copy now describes a concept handoff and does not promise email, postage, tracking, a real locker, or a courier.

Open work: keyboard, screen-reader, focus order, responsive, contrast, and physical kiosk testing. Review generated rationale against real evidence. No card details, real payment, postage label, tracking number, email, or courier booking is created.
